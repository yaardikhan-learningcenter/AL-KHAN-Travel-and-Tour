How currencies work

All books are kept in the PKR base currency.
• Pick a currency on any entry, receipt, payment or expense — it is converted to the base using the rate saved here.
• Use Show in at the top to view the whole app (dashboard, ledgers, P&L) in another currency.
• Invoices can be printed in any currency.
• Changing a rate affects new entries and conversions; saved entries keep the amounts they were recorded with.
